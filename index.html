About

After a Master's in Electrical and Computer Engineering, I followed my curiosity into Design and Multimedia — because I wanted to build things that were not just functional, but felt right.

I've made games entirely from scratch: design, hand-drawn art, code, sound recording and editing. I've built web tools, created motion pieces, animated frame by frame, and modelled in 3D. I also spent 4 years learning not only the Japanese language but also its culture — and worked as a manga translator (English and Spanish to Portuguese).

Today I work as a Data Engineer — but design and making things never stopped.
